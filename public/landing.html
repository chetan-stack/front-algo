// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you hold my money?

No. Your capital stays in your own demat/trading account. In live mode, the bot places orders through your own broker connection — we never receive or move your funds.

What's the difference between paper and live mode?

Paper mode simulates trades on a real-time shared market feed with virtual capital. Live mode places actual orders in your own account and uses market data from your own broker login.

How do I get started?

Click Create account and choose a username and password. Once your account is approved you can sign in and start in paper mode. To trade live, add your own AngelOne or Delta Exchange keys in the Broker Account tab.

Which brokers are supported?

AngelOne for Indian index options and Delta Exchange for crypto.

Can I stop the bot at any time?

Yes. You can turn the auto-strategy off whenever you want. Intraday positions are squared off automatically at 15:10.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. You top up a prepaid wallet (₹50 to ₹1000) and pay only for what you use: ₹5 per live auto order, ₹3 per live auto exit, ₹2 per AI message. Paper trading costs ₹1 per auto order or exit.

What happens when my balance reaches ₹0?

New auto orders pause until you top up. Positions you already have still get their auto exit, so you are never left unprotected.

Can I get my wallet balance back?

No. Wallet credit is used only on TradeSmart AI and can't be withdrawn.

Does the AI guarantee profits?

No. The AI explains what the charts and news are showing; it does not predict the market. All trading decisions and their outcomes remain your responsibility.
